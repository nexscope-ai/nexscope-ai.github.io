Give the model a clear brief

A product video prompt worth starting with.

“Slowly push the camera toward the product on a warm, neutral studio surface. Use soft side lighting. Keep the product centered and finish with a steady close-up of its main detail.”

A prompt idea—not a guarantee of output. Add details specific to your uploaded image.

Specify one movement

Start with a slow push-in or gentle orbit. A focused direction is easier to evaluate than several competing actions.

Keep the setting intentional

Name the surface, lighting and background. Choose a scene that makes the product easy to understand.

Check before publishing

Inspect packaging text, logos, product geometry and claims. Generative video can introduce details that were not in your source image.

AI video generator FAQ

What is an AI video generator from images?

It uses an input image and a text prompt to create a moving scene. Nexscope's tool focuses on ecommerce product photos, with a choice of models and supported video settings.

Can I try it for free?

New Nexscope users receive 1,000 free credits. Generating a video uses credits according to the model and settings. Check the estimate before generating; free credits do not mean unlimited videos.

Do I need an API key?

No API key needs to be entered in the web tool. Sign in with your Nexscope account to upload images and generate. Developer API access has separate requirements.

Can I generate a video from text alone?

This tool is focused on image-to-video generation. Supply the images required by your chosen model, then use text to direct the scene and motion.

Which AI video generator model should I choose?

Choose based on your image inputs, target duration, resolution, aspect ratio and credit estimate. There is no single best model for every product; compare outputs using the same image and prompt.

How long does it take?

Generation runs as a background task. Timing depends on the model, settings and demand. The tool shows progress and the available output when the task completes.

Can I publish the generated videos?

Check applicable terms, rights to your assets and platform disclosure requirements. Review the output for accuracy before publishing. AI-generated examples are not real customer endorsements.
