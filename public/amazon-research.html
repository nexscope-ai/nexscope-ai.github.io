A few things to know.

Does this automatically improve my Amazon ranking?

No. Nexscope supplies research data to support decisions. Rankings and sales depend on many factors, and results are not guaranteed.

Is competitor data my store's actual conversion rate?

No. Provider metrics and estimates are research signals, not your private sales or conversion analytics. Verify results in your own seller reports.

Can I connect this research to an AI agent?

Yes. Supported capabilities are available through API, MCP and Skill access. Check the current API documentation for authentication, coverage and credits.
